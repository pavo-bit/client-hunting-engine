# T010 Owner Decisions — Open Commercial Questions

> Nothing here is answered on the owner's behalf. Each item states why it matters and what can stay provisional. Until decided, the system uses draft catalog entries, operator-entered ranges, and explicit assumptions — never presented as approved offerings.

## 1. Approved service/capability catalog (D-004)

- **Decision needed:** which Grayscales service lines are genuinely deliverable (names, entry criteria, owner).
- **Why:** proposals link capabilities; briefs must label unapproved ones.
- **Provisional:** the two `needs_owner_review` placeholder rows; structure (key/name/criteria/status) is final.

## 2. Deliverable service categories

- **Decision needed:** which project categories to pursue first (portals, integrations, automation, reporting, QA, ecommerce, mobile).
- **Why:** focuses discovery scoring fit and proposal templates.
- **Provisional:** all categories treated equally; no weighting applied.

## 3. Pricing policy (D-005)

- **Decision needed:** may indicative ranges appear in briefs? Rate cards? Margin floors?
- **Why:** commercial sections currently show operator-entered ranges or nothing.
- **Provisional:** ranges-with-assumptions or absent; estimation baselines (S 2–4 … XL 25–40, contingency default 15%) are provisional.

## 4. Currencies and commercial geography

- **Decision needed:** preferred currencies; any geographic commercial focus.
- **Why:** affects quote preparation (no conversion is ever applied automatically).
- **Provisional:** operator-entered 3-letter codes, preserved verbatim.

## 5. Minimum project size

- **Decision needed:** adopt an explicit minimum (effort or value) or deliberately none?
- **Why:** gates trivial pursuits; absence of a floor is itself a decision to record.
- **Provisional:** no minimum enforced.

## 6. Approved claims, portfolio, case studies

- **Decision needed:** which proof points may appear in outreach drafts.
- **Why:** drafts must ground personalization in approved, traceable facts; unverified assertions block approval by design.
- **Provisional:** none — drafts carry explicit unverified-points flags instead.

## 7. Outreach tone and channels

- **Decision needed:** preferred tone and channels (email/DM/form/note).
- **Why:** guides draft preparation; never auto-sends regardless.
- **Provisional:** channel vocabulary only, no tone rules enforced.

## 8. Opportunity qualification requirements

- **Decision needed:** what must be true before human outreach (e.g., validated hypothesis + accepted proposal + approved brief)?
- **Why:** defines pipeline entry criteria.
- **Provisional:** brief creation requires accepted proposals; brief approval requires review; pipeline stages are operator-judged.
